how it works

Three steps, zero uploads

01

Reads your logs

Streams ~/.claude and ~/.codex line by line, dedupes assistant messages, skips broken ones.

02

Does the math

Bundled price list, prefix matching per model, pauses over 30 minutes cut out of agent time. Matches ccusage to the dollar on the same price list.

03

Hands you a PNG

Card in the terminal plus a PNG on disk. No headless browser, no network call, no account.
